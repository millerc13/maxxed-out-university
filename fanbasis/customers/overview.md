# Customers

Everything about your Customers.

## Endpoints

| Method | URL | Description |
|--------|-----|-------------|
| GET | `/public-api/customers` | [Get unique customers](./get-customers.md) |
| GET | `/public-api/customers/:customerId/payment-methods` | [Get customer payment methods](./get-customer-payment-methods.md) |
| POST | `/public-api/customers/:customerId/charge` | [Charge a customer](./charge-customer.md) |
