# Environments

FanBasis has two environments:

- [Production](https://www.fanbasis.com/public-api)
- [Sandbox](https://qa.dev-fan-basis.com/)

## Production

Production is the default, live environment. All payments are live and will be processed by FanBasis.

**Base URL:** `https://www.fanbasis.com/public-api`

## Sandbox
