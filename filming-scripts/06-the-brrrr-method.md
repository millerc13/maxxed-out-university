# LESSON 1: BUY: Finding Properties That Work for BRRRR

---

## SCRIPT

BRRRR starts with buying the right property. Not every deal works for this strategy.

## What Makes a Good BRRRR Deal?

### 1. Significant Discount
You need to buy below market value — ideally 70-80% of ARV minus rehab. This creates the equity you'll pull out later.

### 2. Value-Add Opportunity
The property needs work. That's how you force appreciation. Cosmetic rehabs are easier; heavy rehabs have higher potential.

### 3. Strong Rental Market
After rehab, it needs to rent quickly at market rates. Research rent comps before buying.

### 4. Refinance Potential
The ARV needs to be high enough that you can refinance and pull most (or all) of your capital back out.

## The BRRRR Math

**Goal:** Pull 100% (or close) of your investment back out at refinance.

### Example
- Purchase: $80,000
- Rehab: $30,000
- Closing/Holding: $10,000
- **Total Investment: $120,000**

- ARV: $180,000
- Refinance at 75% LTV: $135,000
- **Cash Back: $135,000**
- **Money Left in Deal: $0** (Actually pulled out $15K extra!)

### The Key Numbers
- Buy at deep discount
- Rehab to maximize ARV
- Refinance pulls out your capital
- Repeat with recycled money

## Finding BRRRR Deals

Best sources:
- Off-market (driving for dollars, direct mail)
- Wholesalers
- Auction properties
- REO/bank-owned

MLS deals CAN work, but competition makes deep discounts harder.

# LESSON 2: REHAB: Renovating for Rentals (Not HGTV)

---

## SCRIPT

BRRRR rehabs are different from flip rehabs. You're building for durability and rent, not for maximum sale price.

## Rental-Grade vs Flip-Grade Finishes

### Flip-Grade (DON'T do this for BRRRR)
- Granite countertops
- Stainless steel appliances
- Custom tile backsplash
- Hardwood floors
- High-end fixtures

### Rental-Grade (DO this for BRRRR)
- Laminate or butcher block countertops
- Basic black or white appliances
- Simple subway tile or paint backsplash
- LVP (luxury vinyl plank) flooring
- Standard fixtures

## Why Rental-Grade?

1. **Lower cost** — Spend less, keep more equity
2. **Durability** — LVP survives tenants better than hardwood
3. **Easy replacement** — Standard items are cheaper to fix
4. **Tenants don't pay more** — Premium finishes rarely increase rent significantly

## What to Focus On

### Always Fix
- Safety issues (electrical, structural, roof)
- Functionality (HVAC, plumbing, appliances)
- Curb appeal (paint, landscaping)
- Clean, neutral appearance

### Don't Overspend On
- Custom anything
- Trendy finishes
- Luxury upgrades
- Things tenants will destroy

## Common BRRRR Rehab Mistakes

1. **Over-improving** — Spending $50K when $30K would rent for the same
2. **Ignoring mechanicals** — Roof and HVAC problems kill cash flow
3. **Bad contractors** — Delays destroy your timeline
4. **No contingency** — Surprises always happen

# LESSON 3: RENT: Attracting & Screening Great Tenants

---

## SCRIPT

A BRRRR deal isn't complete until it's rented. And the tenant you choose determines your cash flow and headaches for years.

## Marketing the Property

### Where to List
- Zillow (free)
- Facebook Marketplace (free)
- Craigslist (free)
- Apartments.com (paid)
- Your property manager's network

### What Makes a Good Listing
- High-quality photos (bright, clean, wide angles)
- Complete information (beds, baths, sqft, amenities)
- Clear pricing
- Application instructions

## Pre-Screening (Before Showing)

Ask these questions upfront:
1. When are you looking to move?
2. How many people will be living there?
3. Do you have pets?
4. What is your monthly income?
5. Have you ever been evicted?

This saves time by filtering out unqualified applicants.

## Full Application Process

### What to Collect
- Completed application
- Government ID
- Proof of income (pay stubs, tax returns, bank statements)
- Rental history (previous landlord contact info)
- Authorization to run background/credit check

### What to Check
- **Credit score:** Look for patterns, not just the number
- **Income:** Should be 3x monthly rent
- **Rental history:** Call previous landlords
- **Criminal background:** Follow fair housing laws
- **Eviction history:** Major red flag

## Red Flags

🚩 Can't verify income
🚩 Multiple evictions
🚩 Won't provide references
🚩 Wants to move in immediately with cash
🚩 Tries to negotiate application requirements

## Fair Housing Reminder

You CANNOT discriminate based on:
- Race, color, national origin
- Religion
- Sex, familial status
- Disability

Apply the same criteria to every applicant, consistently.

# LESSON 4: REFINANCE: Getting Your Money Back

---

## SCRIPT

The refinance is where BRRRR magic happens. This is how you pull your capital back out to do it again.

## When to Refinance

### Seasoning Requirements
Most lenders require 6-12 months of ownership before refinancing. Some DSCR lenders have no seasoning.

### Property Must Be Stabilized
- Rehab complete
- Tenant in place
- Rent being collected

## How Appraisers Value the Property

For rental properties, appraisers typically use:

1. **Comparable Sales** — What similar properties sold for
2. **Income Approach** — NOI and cap rate (more common for 5+ units)

### Maximizing Your Appraisal
- Provide list of improvements made
- Share rent comps showing market rates
- Make sure property is clean for appraiser visit
- Highlight any value-adds (extra bedroom, garage, etc.)

## Cash-Out vs Rate/Term Refinance

### Cash-Out Refinance
Pull equity out as cash. This is what BRRRR investors want.
- Typically limited to 75% LTV
- Slightly higher rates than rate/term

### Rate/Term Refinance
Just change the loan terms (rate, length) without taking cash out.
- Can go up to 80% LTV
- Lower rates

## DSCR Loans for BRRRR

DSCR loans are perfect for BRRRR because:
- No personal income verification
- Based on property cash flow
- Some have no seasoning requirements
- Close in 2-3 weeks

## Risks to Watch

### Overleveraging
Don't pull out so much that you have negative cash flow. Leave some equity cushion.

### Rising Rates
If rates increase between purchase and refinance, your numbers change. Stress test your deals.

### Appraisal Comes in Low
If ARV is lower than expected, you can't pull out as much. Have backup capital.

# LESSON 5: REPEAT: Building a BRRRR Portfolio

---

## SCRIPT

The "Repeat" in BRRRR is where wealth is built. Here's how to scale from one deal to many.

## How Repeat Works

1. Complete BRRRR #1
2. Pull capital back out at refinance
3. Use that capital for BRRRR #2
4. Repeat

### Example Growth

**Year 1:**
- Start with $50K
- BRRRR Deal #1: $50K in, pull $50K out
- Now have: 1 rental + $50K cash

**Year 2:**
- BRRRR Deal #2: $50K in, pull $50K out
- BRRRR Deal #3: $50K in, pull $50K out
- Now have: 3 rentals + $50K cash

**Year 3:**
- Add 2-3 more properties
- Now have: 5-6 rentals generating cash flow

The same $50K just keeps recycling while you accumulate properties.

## Scaling: Slow vs Aggressive

### Conservative Approach
- 1-2 BRRRRs per year
- Fully stabilize before moving on
- Build reserves between deals
- Lower risk, steady growth

### Aggressive Approach
- 3-5+ BRRRRs per year
- Multiple projects running simultaneously
- Requires more capital, team, systems
- Higher risk, faster growth

**Recommendation:** Start conservative. Speed up once you have systems and experience.

## Building Systems Around BRRRR

To scale, you need:

### Team
- Reliable contractors
- Property manager (or VA for self-management)
- Lender relationships (hard money + refinance)
- Real estate attorney

### Processes
- Deal analysis template
- Rehab scope of work template
- Tenant screening criteria
- Refinance checklist

### Tracking
- Spreadsheet or software for all properties
- Monthly P&L per property
- Portfolio-wide metrics

## The Long Game

10 properties × $200/month cash flow = $2,000/month

20 properties × $200/month = $4,000/month

40 properties × $200/month = $8,000/month

**That's how BRRRR builds wealth.**
