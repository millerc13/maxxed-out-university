# COURSE OVERVIEW

The complete real estate investing masterclass. Learn how to build wealth through real estate with Todd Pultz, who went from a trailer park and foreclosure to over 1,000+ rental units. This comprehensive 10-module course covers everything from foundations and deal analysis to BRRRR, flipping, wholesaling, and scaling your portfolio into a real business.
