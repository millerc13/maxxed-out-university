# LESSON 1: Building Your Power Team

---

## SCRIPT

You can't scale alone. Building a reliable team is essential for growth.

## Your Core Team Members

### 1. Property Manager
- Handles day-to-day tenant issues
- Collects rent, coordinates maintenance
- Critical for scaling rental portfolio

### 2. CPA (Accountant)
- Tax strategy and planning
- Entity structure advice
- Quarterly and annual filings
- Find one who specializes in real estate investors

### 3. Real Estate Attorney
- Contract review
- Entity formation
- Evictions (if needed)
- Creative deal structuring

### 4. Real Estate Agents
- MLS access and comps
- Deal sourcing
- Selling your flips
- Build relationships with 2-3 investor-friendly agents

### 5. Contractors
- General contractor for big jobs
- Specialists: plumber, electrician, HVAC
- Handyman for small repairs
- Build your list over time through referrals

### 6. Lenders
- Hard money lender for acquisitions
- Private money contacts
- DSCR or portfolio lender for refinances
- Conventional lender for best rates

### 7. Insurance Agent
- Landlord policies
- Flip/renovation coverage
- Umbrella policies
- Should understand investor needs

### 8. Bookkeeper
- Track income and expenses
- Reconcile accounts
- Prepare reports for CPA
- Can be part-time or virtual

## What to Look For in Team Members

### Experience with Investors
They should understand your business model, not just residential homeowners.

### Responsiveness
If they take days to return calls, find someone else. Speed matters in this business.

### Fair Pricing
Not always the cheapest, but transparent and reasonable.

### Long-Term Mindset
You want partners who grow with you, not one-time transactions.

## Building Relationships

- Pay promptly
- Refer them business
- Be easy to work with
- Communicate clearly
- Show appreciation

**Your team is an asset. Invest in those relationships.**

# LESSON 2: Systems & KPIs

---

## SCRIPT

"What gets measured gets managed." To scale, you need systems and numbers to track.

## Why Systems Matter

Without systems:
- Everything depends on you
- You make the same mistakes repeatedly
- You can't delegate
- Growth is limited by your time

With systems:
- Processes are documented
- Others can execute
- Results are consistent
- You can scale

## Core Systems to Build

### 1. Deal Analysis System
- Standardized spreadsheet or software
- Same criteria for every deal
- Go/no-go checklist

### 2. Lead Management System
- CRM or spreadsheet
- Track source, status, follow-ups
- Nothing falls through cracks

### 3. Project Management System
- Rehab timeline templates
- Contractor payment tracking
- Progress documentation

### 4. Property Management System
- Tenant screening process
- Maintenance request flow
- Rent collection process

### 5. Financial Tracking System
- Income and expenses by property
- Monthly and annual reports
- Cash flow tracking

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

### Lead & Deal KPIs
- **Leads per month:** How many leads are you generating?
- **Lead-to-contract ratio:** What percentage become deals?
- **Cost per lead:** How much are you spending per lead?
- **Cost per deal:** Total marketing cost ÷ deals closed

### Financial KPIs
- **Monthly cash flow:** Total rental income minus expenses
- **Cash-on-cash return:** By property and portfolio
- **Portfolio NOI:** Total net operating income

### Operational KPIs
- **Vacancy rate:** % of time units are empty
- **Average days to fill:** How long to lease a vacant unit
- **Average rehab timeline:** Days from purchase to completion
- **Average rehab budget variance:** Actual vs estimated costs

## Tracking Your KPIs

### Weekly Review
- Leads generated
- Deals analyzed
- Offers made
- Contracts signed

### Monthly Review
- Cash flow by property
- Vacancy status
- Project progress
- Budget vs actual

### Quarterly Review
- Portfolio performance
- KPI trends
- Goal progress
- Strategy adjustments

**Start simple.** Track a few key numbers consistently, then add more as you grow.

# LESSON 3: Todd's 1,000+ Unit Strategy (Case Study)

---

## SCRIPT

I didn't start with 1,000 units. I started with one deal and a lot of determination. Here's how it happened.

## The Starting Point

**2011:** Rock bottom. Foreclosure, repossession, credit under 500.

**2012:** First deal. A small single-family rental. Seller-financed because no bank would touch me.

That one deal changed everything. Not because of the money (it barely cash flowed), but because of the belief it created.

## The Early Years (Years 1-3)

### Strategy
- Buy distressed single-family homes
- Light rehab
- Rent and hold
- Reinvest all cash flow

### Key Moves
- Found a private money lender (family friend) who believed in me
- Focused on one small market I knew well
- Did most rehab work myself to save money
- Learned everything I could (books, podcasts, mentors)

### Results
- 10 units by end of Year 3
- Still working my W2 job
- Building credibility with lenders

## The Growth Phase (Years 4-7)

### Strategy
- BRRRR to recycle capital
- Added small multifamily (2-4 units)
- Started raising private money
- Hired first property manager

### Key Moves
- Quit my job (scary but necessary)
- Built systems for acquisition and management
- Started building a team
- Focused on deal flow over everything else

### Results
- 50+ units by end of Year 5
- 150+ units by end of Year 7
- Multiple private lenders
- Full-time team in place

## The Scale Phase (Years 8+)

### Strategy
- Larger multifamily (20+ units)
- Diversified into other businesses (Medicaid services)
- Focus on leadership and vision, not day-to-day

### Key Moves
- Partnered with investors for larger deals
- Hired executives to run operations
- Focused on deal selection, not deal execution
- Started teaching and giving back

### Results
- 1,000+ units
- Multiple businesses
- Time freedom
- Ability to help others do the same

## Lessons Learned

### 1. Start Small, Start Now
Don't wait for the perfect deal or perfect circumstances. My first deal was ugly. Yours might be too. Do it anyway.

### 2. Reinvest Everything Early
I lived below my means and put every dollar back into deals. That compounding is how you build real wealth.

### 3. Relationships Are Everything
Private lenders, partners, contractors, property managers — my network made the growth possible.

### 4. Mistakes Are Tuition
I've lost money on deals. I've hired wrong. I've made bad decisions. Every one of them taught me something.

### 5. Think Bigger Than You're Comfortable With
When I had 10 units, 100 seemed impossible. When I had 100, 500 seemed impossible. Your ceiling is usually in your head.

# LESSON 4: Your 12-Month Roadmap

---

## SCRIPT

You've learned the strategies. Now it's time to build YOUR plan. Here's a realistic 12-month roadmap.

## Months 1-3: Foundation

### Focus: Learn and Prepare

**Month 1:**
- Complete this course
- Define your investor profile (Builder/Operator/Hybrid)
- Choose your market
- Start building your team list

**Month 2:**
- Analyze 30+ deals (practice underwriting)
- Attend 2+ local REI meetups
- Interview 3+ real estate agents
- Interview 2+ property managers
- Connect with 5+ potential private lenders

**Month 3:**
- Set up your lead sources (pick 2-3)
- Create your deal analysis system
- Make your first offers (even if they don't get accepted)
- Keep analyzing deals daily

### Goal: Be ready to execute, with team and systems in place.

## Months 4-6: First Deal

### Focus: Execute Your First Deal

**Month 4:**
- Increase lead generation
- Make multiple offers per week
- Follow up on every lead
- Get your first property under contract

**Month 5:**
- Complete due diligence
- Secure financing
- Close on your first deal
- Begin rehab (if applicable)

**Month 6:**
- Complete rehab OR
- Get property rented OR
- Assign contract to buyer
- Document everything you learn

### Goal: Close your first deal. Learn from the experience.

## Months 7-9: Refine and Repeat

### Focus: Improve Your Process

**Month 7:**
- Debrief your first deal (what worked, what didn't)
- Adjust your systems based on lessons learned
- Continue lead generation
- Analyze your KPIs

**Month 8:**
- Execute deal #2
- Refine your contractor relationships
- Build your buyers list (if wholesaling)
- Strengthen your funding sources

**Month 9:**
- Work toward deal #3
- Start building documentation for your processes
- Evaluate: Is your strategy working? Adjust if needed.

### Goal: Develop your repeatable process through multiple deals.

## Months 10-12: Scale

### Focus: Build Momentum

**Month 10:**
- Increase marketing/lead generation budget
- Work on deals #3-4
- Hire help where needed (VA, bookkeeper, etc.)
- Review and strengthen your team

**Month 11:**
- Push toward 4+ deals by year end
- Develop your 12-month plan for Year 2
- Consider expanding your buy box or market
- Build relationships for larger deals

**Month 12:**
- Review your year: deals done, cash flow, lessons learned
- Celebrate your wins
- Set goals for Year 2
- Keep the momentum going

### Goal: End the year with multiple deals done and systems for growth.

## Your Personalized Plan

Adjust this roadmap based on your:
- **Starting capital** — Less money = more creative strategies
- **Time availability** — Less time = slower pace, more leverage
- **Risk tolerance** — Lower risk = more conservative deals
- **Market conditions** — Hot market vs buyer's market

**The key is consistent action. Show up every day, even when it's hard.**
